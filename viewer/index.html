      Neuron sweep, labels and median residual norms from nikhilxvytla/neuron-atlas (ODC-BY). Each neuron gets dose × median(‖resid‖) / ‖W_down[:, n]‖ added at its down_proj input on every token, greedy, 32 tokens.
      
    
      Kitchen recipes come from Neuronary (CC0) and are credited to their authors. --mix applies every dose evenly on every token. Atlas labels are written by an LLM and describe what a neuron fires on, which is often not what pushing it does.
